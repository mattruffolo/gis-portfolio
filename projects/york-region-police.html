York Region Police Emergency Response Coverage

A drive-time coverage model identifying where response targets aren't met across York Region, quantified by population and ranked by municipality.

Tools ArcGIS Pro ArcGIS Online Experience Builder FME Workbench
Skills Network analysis Service-area modelling Census DA processing Spatial joins Dashboard design
TypeCoverage analysis
GeographyYork Region, Ontario
OutputExperience Builder web app

Objective

York Regional Police operate from a fixed set of divisional stations across a region that mixes dense urban centres with large low-density areas. The question this project answers: which parts of the region fall outside target drive-time coverage, how many residents live there, and which municipalities are most exposed?

Data

  • Station locations — York Regional Police divisional and district facilities, geocoded.
  • Road network — routing network used to generate drive-time service areas.
  • Dissemination Areas — Statistics Canada DAs with population counts, clipped to York Region.
  • Municipal boundaries — Census Subdivisions for the nine municipalities in the region.

Method

  1. Input preparation — loaded station points, DA polygons, and municipal boundaries; reprojected everything to a common coordinate system so area and distance calculations were valid.
  2. DA centroid creation — converted DA polygons to representative points so each area could be assigned a single, unambiguous coverage result.
  3. Service-area generation — built drive-time polygons around each station at the target response thresholds.
  4. Coverage assignment — spatially joined DA centroids to the service-area bands, flagging each DA as covered or outside coverage.
  5. Regional statistics — aggregated population by coverage band to quantify how many residents sit outside target.
  6. Municipality rankings — rolled coverage up to the municipal level and ranked municipalities by share of population uncovered.
  7. Publish — pushed the DA coverage output to ArcGIS Online and built the front end in Experience Builder.

Outcome & takeaway

The app makes gaps visible at a glance and quantifies them in population terms rather than just area — which is the framing that matters for a service decision. The same structure transfers directly to commercial work: swap stations for store locations and the model becomes a trade-area coverage and white-space analysis.
