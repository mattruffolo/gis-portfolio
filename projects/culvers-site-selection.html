Culver's Canadian Growth Strategy — A Mock Site Selection Analysis

A mock market-entry study scoring candidate trade areas on demographics, accessibility, and competitor saturation to rank where Culver's should open first.

Tools ArcGIS StoryMaps QGIS
Skills Suitability modelling Hex tessellation scoring Demographic enrichment Competitor analysis Trade-area definition
TypeSite selection
GeographyMilton
OutputArcGIS StoryMap

Objective

If Culver's were expanding into Canada, where should the first location go? The analysis scores candidate areas on customer fit, accessibility, and competitive pressure, then ranks them based on their site score.

Data

  • Demographics — Statistics Canada census (2021) variables: population, household income, age structure, household size.
  • Competitors — QSR locations extracted from Overpass Turbo,.
  • Traffic & accessibility — major road network and traffic indicators.
  • Hexagonal grid — hexagonal tessellation covering the study area, used as the scoring unit.

Outcome

The retail site selection analysis that was conducted concluded the top 3 locations for Culver's first Canadian restaurant locations. It is supported by real-world, publicly available data where the weighting is transparent and adjustable. This type of analysis enables a stakeholder who disagrees with an assumption to see exactly what changes if the weightings are changed.
