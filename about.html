About me

I'm a Location Intelligence Associate with CBRE Canada, based out of the Toronto West office in Mississauga. My day-to-day is spatial analysis and mapping that supports brokerage and advisory teams — site and trade-area analysis, demographic profiling, amenities and transit mapping for marketing materials, and custom web maps and dashboards.

The three projects on this site came out of my upskilling program. I chose them deliberately to cover different ground: a public sector coverage study, a production automation build, and a private sector retail expansion analysis. Together they show the range of problems location intelligence can answer, not just one technique repeated three times.
